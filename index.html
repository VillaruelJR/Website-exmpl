How It Works

Look through found items or submit a lost item report.

3

Verify Ownership

Visit the office with your school ID and describe the item.

4

Claim Your Item

Sign the claim form and take your item home.

What to Bring When Claiming

  • Valid school ID or government ID
  • Proof of ownership (photo, receipt, or details)
  • Reference number from your report

Good to Know

  • Items are kept for 60 days
  • Unclaimed items may be donated or disposed of
  • Valuables are stored securely in the office

Frequently Asked Questions

Can someone else claim my item for me?

Yes, with an authorization letter, a copy of your ID, and their own valid ID.

How long are found items kept?

Items are stored for 60 days from the date they were turned in.

I don't see my item. What should I do?

Submit a lost item report. We will contact you if a matching item is turned in.

Where do I hand in an item I found?

Bring it to the Lost & Found office or any security guard, then fill out the "I Found Something" form.
